# SEO upgrade: October 2026

Branch `seo/2026-10-search-upgrade`, based on `main` after PR #15. Nothing has
been deployed and no Business Profile or directory changes were made.

## What the research found

Researched on 10 October 2026. Sources are listed at the end.

- **AI search is still SEO.** Google's guide to its generative AI features
  (15 May 2026) says AI Overviews and AI Mode draw on the same index and
  quality systems as normal results. It lists `llms.txt` and AI-specific
  markup as things you can skip. Other AI assistants may still read
  `llms.txt`, so the site now serves one, but it is not a Google lever.
- **FAQ rich results are gone.** Google stopped showing FAQ rich results on
  7 May 2026. FAQ schema stays (it is valid and harmless), but no new FAQ
  markup was added for search appearance.
- **Local pack rankings are driven mostly off the website.** Industry surveys
  put the Google Business Profile as the biggest local-pack factor: primary
  category, reviews, accurate hours and consistent business details across
  directories. The service-area list helps customers but does not widen
  ranking reach.
- **Location pages need real local substance.** Google's doorway policy
  targets near-identical city pages. Each suburb page already had its own
  housing profile; this pass adds council-specific permit links and
  suburb-specific descriptions instead of more templated copy.
- **How people search (Google Australia autocomplete):** "kitchen / bathroom
  renovations melbourne **western suburbs**", "deck builders melbourne
  western suburbs", "**house painters** melbourne", "home extensions melbourne
  **cost**", "second storey extension melbourne cost", "granny flat victoria
  **rules** / **no permit**", "small second dwelling victoria", and
  service-plus-suburb searches such as "bathroom renovations cranbourne".
- **Rules that changed since the guides were written:**
  - **Home Warranty** replaced domestic building insurance for contracts
    signed on or after 1 July 2026. It applies to eligible work over $20,000
    (DBI was over $16,000).
  - **Amendment VC282** (from 8 September 2025): small second dwellings on
    residential lots under 300 m² need a planning permit assessed against
    Clause 54.
  - Greater Dandenong and Hobsons Bay have paused new council building permit
    applications, and Casey, Melton and Cardinia point homeowners to private
    building surveyors. The suburb pages told people they could use their
    council's municipal building surveyor, which was wrong for many of them.

## New home builds (13 October 2026)

A new service page, `/new-home-builders-melbourne`, targets the main searches
from Google Australia autocomplete: "new home builders melbourne", "home
builders western suburbs melbourne", "knockdown rebuild melbourne (cost /
builders)", "custom home builders melbourne", "double storey home builders
melbourne" and "cost to build a house melbourne". It is featured above the
service grid on the homepage, listed first (with a "New" tag) in the menus,
footer, enquiry form and suburb pages, and included in the sitemap, schema and
`llms.txt`.

- **Images** in `public/images/new-homes/` are AI-generated examples
  (disclosed on `/terms#imagery`). They stay `unverified` in
  `src/content/images.ts`, so the gallery labels them as example images.
  Swap in real job photos (same file names) once projects are photographed.
- **Pricing** uses indicative 2026 Melbourne ranges from published cost
  guides. The knockdown rebuild range on the extensions page and in the
  extension cost guide now matches ($400,000–$850,000+).
- **Rules cited** (7-star NatHERS, all-electric from 1 January 2027, Home
  Warranty) come from Planning Victoria, Sustainability Victoria and the BPC.
- **Owner:** building new homes is domestic building work that needs a
  registered builder. The page doesn't claim registration until the
  `bpcRegistration` claim is approved with evidence. Remove the "Duplex &
  dual occupancy" type if Konntey doesn't take on those projects.

## Owner checklist (off-site, biggest impact first)

1. **Make the www redirect permanent.** `konnteyhomerenovations.com.au`
   currently redirects to `www` with a **307 (temporary)**. In Vercel, open
   Project → Settings → Domains, edit the apex domain and set the redirect to
   **308 Permanent**. The permanent rule in `vercel.json` is not what visitors
   get today; the domain setting answers first with the 307.
2. **Google Business Profile.** Set it up as a service-area business, with no
   address shown unless customers visit. Choose the primary category
   carefully, because it gates which searches you can appear in. A general
   renovation category fits, with Kitchen remodeler, Bathroom remodeler, Deck
   builder and Painter as secondary categories. Then:
   - Add every service with plain names that match the site (for example
     "Kitchen renovations", "Granny flats").
   - Keep hours accurate.
   - Link the website with a tracking parameter, for example
     `?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp`.
   - Post real job photos.
3. **Reviews.** Ask every finished client for a Google review, and reply to
   each one. Put the "ask for reviews" link in `googleReviewUrl`
   (`src/content/business.ts`). Never script or incentivise reviews. Reviews
   that naturally mention the job and suburb help both Maps and AI answers.
4. **Free directory listings with identical details.** Use exactly *Konntey
   Home & Renovations*, 0493 191 798 and the www address on Apple Business
   Connect, Bing Places, Facebook, Yellow Pages and True Local (one Thryv
   sign-up), Yelp, Word of Mouth, ProductReview.com.au, Localsearch, Hotfrog,
   Cylex and Brownbook. Consistent details are a ranking factor for Maps and
   for AI assistants. hipages, ServiceSeeking, Bark and Houzz are paid lead or
   subscription services, and Oneflare closed on 30 June 2026.
5. **Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.** Resubmit `sitemap.xml` after
   deploying and request indexing for the two new guides. Add the site to Bing
   Webmaster Tools (import from Search Console). Bing feeds several AI
   assistants.
6. **Trust content.** Approving claims with evidence (section 5 of
   `OWNER-CONTENT-GUIDE.md`), publishing real project case studies and adding
   a named author to the guides are the strongest remaining on-site signals.
   Approved claims flow into the copy, meta descriptions and schema
   automatically.
7. **Local links.** Supplier and trade-partner pages, industry association
   directories if you join one, local community sponsorships.
8. **Yearly upkeep.** Re-check the council links (`src/content/councils.ts`)
   and the regulatory facts in the guides, and update their `dateModified`
   when you do.

## Deliberately not done

- **No service × suburb pages** (for example "kitchen renovations Tarneit").
  At 19 suburbs × 6 services, they would be the near-duplicate pages Google's
  doorway policy describes.
- **No review or rating schema.** There are no approved reviews, and
  self-published business reviews are not eligible for review stars anyway.
- **No AI-specific rewriting or markup**, in line with Google's May 2026
  guidance.

## How to measure

- **Search Console → Performance (Web).** Compare 28 days before and after
  deploying, filtered on queries containing "western suburbs", "granny flat",
  "extension cost" and suburb names, and on pages under `/guides/`. Clicks
  from AI Overviews and AI Mode are counted here too.
- **Business Profile performance:** calls, website clicks and direction
  requests.
- **Enquiries:** the existing analytics events (`quote_form_submit`,
  `phone_click`) once GA4 or GTM is connected.
